GRID ARENA

You start in the village of Gridholm: talk to the locals, trade in the shops, then take the stairs by the north gate down into the dungeon. The dungeon is a grid of labyrinths. Stairwells lead to neighbouring sectors and the hatch takes you deeper. Some passages are sealed by locked doors. Defeat the guardian before each gate to get an Access Key. Loot goes into your backpack, and relics only work once equipped in a module.
